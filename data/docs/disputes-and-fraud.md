# Disputes and unauthorised payments

## Payments you didn't make
If you see a payment you don't recognise, freeze your card in the app first, then report the payment with "I don't recognise this". Our fraud specialists review reports within 1 business day. For confirmed unauthorised payments we refund the amount, usually within 5 business days of confirmation.

## Someone accessed your account
If you think someone else has signed in to your account, reset your password immediately and sign out all devices under Settings > Security. Then contact us so we can review recent activity and secure the account.

## Disputing a purchase with a merchant
If a merchant didn't deliver, charged you twice or charged the wrong amount, contact the merchant first. If they don't resolve it within 14 days, open a dispute from the transaction screen. You can dispute card payments up to 120 days after the transaction date. Disputes are decided by the card network and can take up to 45 days.

## Scams and phishing
Kestrel will never ask for your PIN, password or one-time codes, and will never ask you to move money to a "safe account". If you clicked a suspicious link or shared details, freeze your card, change your password and contact us right away.
