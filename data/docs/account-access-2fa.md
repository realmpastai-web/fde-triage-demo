# Signing in, two-step verification and locked accounts

## Locked after too many attempts
After 5 incorrect password attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it. You can unlock it sooner by resetting your password from the sign-in screen with "Forgot password".

## Verification codes not arriving
SMS codes can take up to 2 minutes. Check that your phone has signal and that the number on your account is correct. You can switch to app-based codes under Settings > Security. If you no longer have access to your phone number, choose "I can't access my phone" on the sign-in screen to start identity recovery.

## New phone or reinstalled app
When you sign in on a new device we ask you to confirm it with a selfie check and a code sent to your registered email. The old device is signed out automatically.

## Password reset emails
Reset emails come from no-reply@kestrel.example and expire after 1 hour. Check your spam folder. If you no longer use the email on your account, start identity recovery instead.

## Restricted accounts
We may restrict an account while we review unusual activity or missing information. The app shows what we need from you. Restrictions are usually lifted within 2 business days of receiving the information.
