# Fees, plans and refunds of fees

## Plans
Kestrel Standard is free. Kestrel Plus costs 9.99 USD per month and includes fee-free ATM withdrawals up to 800 USD a month, express card delivery and higher spending limits.

## ATM fees
Standard customers get 3 fee-free ATM withdrawals per month, up to 200 USD in total. After that, each withdrawal costs 2% with a minimum of 1 USD. Some ATM operators add their own fee, which we do not control and cannot refund.

## Currency exchange
Payments in another currency are converted at the interbank rate. On weekends a 1% markup applies to Standard customers because markets are closed. Plus customers have no weekend markup.

## Cancelling Plus
You can cancel Plus at any time under Account > Plan. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing month and you will not be charged again. Plan fees already charged are not refundable for a partial month.

## Disputing a fee
If you think a fee was charged in error, reply with the date and amount. A Billing specialist reviews every fee dispute; we will tell you the outcome within 5 business days. Support agents cannot promise a refund before that review.
