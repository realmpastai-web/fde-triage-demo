# Accounts, savings and features

## Who can open an account
Anyone aged 18 or over who lives in a supported country can open a personal account in the app in about 10 minutes.

## Joint and business accounts
Joint accounts are not available yet. Business accounts are available for sole traders and registered companies through Kestrel Business, which has its own sign-up in the app.

## Savings pots
Savings pots let you set money aside from your main balance. Pots currently earn 3.1% AER variable interest, paid monthly. You can move money in and out of pots instantly.

## Closing your account
You can close your account under Account > Close account once your balance is zero and there are no pending transactions. We keep records for 5 years after closure, as required by law.

## Feedback
We read every piece of feedback. Suggestions are shared with our product team every week.
