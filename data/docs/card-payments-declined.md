# Why was my card payment declined?

## Common reasons
Most declines happen because the card is frozen, the balance is too low, the payment is over your daily spending limit, or the merchant category is blocked in your card settings. The decline reason is shown on the transaction in the app.

## Spending limits
Unverified accounts have a daily card limit of 500 USD. Verified Standard accounts have 3,000 USD per day and Plus accounts 10,000 USD per day. You can lower your own limits at any time under Card > Limits.

## Paying abroad and at ATMs
Kestrel cards work in over 150 countries. Some ATMs and merchants abroad only accept chip and PIN, so a contactless or magnetic-stripe payment may fail. If an ATM declines your card, try another ATM and check that "ATM withdrawals" is switched on under Card > Security.

## Contactless problems
After you receive a new card, make one chip and PIN payment before using contactless. If contactless still fails, the card's antenna may be damaged and we can send a free replacement.

## Card expiry
We send a new card automatically about 30 days before your card expires. Your card number stays the same and only the expiry date and security code change.
