# Lost or stolen card

## Freeze your card straight away
If you can't find your card, freeze it in the app under Card > Freeze. Freezing is instant and reversible, so it is the safest first step even if you think the card is only misplaced. Frozen cards decline all card payments and ATM withdrawals, but direct debits and incoming transfers keep working.

## Order a replacement
If the card is definitely lost or stolen, choose Card > Report lost or stolen. This permanently cancels the old card and orders a replacement to your registered address. Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business days; express delivery takes 2 business days for Plus customers. Your virtual card is reissued instantly so you can keep paying online and with your phone.

## If someone used your card
If you see payments you did not make, report them from the transaction screen with "I don't recognise this". Our fraud specialists review these within 1 business day. See "Disputes and unauthorised payments" for how refunds of unauthorised payments work.

## Never share your PIN
Kestrel will never ask for your full card number, PIN, password or a one-time code by email, chat or phone.
