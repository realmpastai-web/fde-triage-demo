# Transfers, pending payments and missing money

## How long transfers take
Domestic instant transfers usually arrive within minutes. Standard domestic bank transfers take up to 1 business day. International transfers take 1 to 4 business days depending on the destination and intermediary banks. Transfers made after 5 pm or on weekends are processed the next business day.

## Pending transfers
A transfer shows as pending while it is being checked by us or the receiving bank. Most pending transfers complete within 24 hours. If a transfer has been pending for more than 2 business days, contact us with the transfer reference and we will trace it.

## Failed transfers and refunds to your balance
If a transfer fails, the money is returned to your Kestrel balance automatically, normally within 3 business days. The transfer will show as "Returned" in your activity.

## Sent to the wrong account
We can't reverse a transfer once the receiving bank has accepted it, but we can request a recall from the receiving bank. Recalls are not guaranteed and can take up to 10 business days. Report a mistaken transfer as soon as possible from the transfer screen.

## Missing incoming payments
Ask the sender for the payment reference and the date it was sent, and check that they used your Kestrel account details exactly as shown under Account > Details. Incoming international payments can take up to 4 business days.
