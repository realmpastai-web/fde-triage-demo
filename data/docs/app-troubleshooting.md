# App troubleshooting

## App crashes or freezes
Update to the latest version of the Kestrel app from your app store, then restart your phone. If the app still crashes, sign out and back in, or reinstall it. Your money and data are safe: reinstalling does not affect your account.

## Statements and exports
Monthly statements are available as PDF under Account > Statements. CSV exports cover up to 12 months at a time. If an export fails, try a shorter date range.

## Notifications
Check that notifications are allowed for Kestrel in your phone's settings and in the app under Settings > Notifications. Battery saver modes can delay notifications.

## Supported devices
The app supports iOS 16 and later and Android 10 and later. Older versions may still open but are not tested.

## Reporting a bug
When you report a bug, tell us your phone model, operating system version, app version (Settings > About) and the steps that cause the problem. Screenshots help, but please crop out any card numbers.
