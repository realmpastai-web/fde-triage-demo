# Identity verification (KYC)

## Why we verify your identity
As a regulated payments provider we must verify every customer before lifting account limits. Until you are verified, you can hold up to 1,000 USD and spend up to 500 USD a day.

## Accepted documents
We accept a valid passport, national ID card or driving licence. For proof of address we accept a utility bill, bank statement or government letter dated within the last 3 months. Screenshots and expired documents are not accepted.

## Tips for a successful upload
Photograph the whole document on a dark, flat surface in good light, with no glare and all four corners visible. Your selfie must match the photo on your document, without hats or sunglasses.

## How long verification takes
Most verifications complete within 10 minutes. Some need a manual review by a compliance specialist, which takes up to 2 business days. If your verification has been in review for longer than 2 business days, contact us and we will check its status.

## Rejected documents
If a document is rejected the app tells you why. The most common reasons are blur, glare, cropped corners and an expired document. You can try again up to 3 times before a specialist reviews your case.
