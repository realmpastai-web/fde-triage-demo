# Kestrel support reply policy (client-provided)

Applied to every draft before a human sees it. Violations block the draft.

1. Never ask the customer for a full card number, CVV/security code, PIN, password or one-time code.
2. Never promise a refund, reversal or outcome that depends on a specialist review ("we will refund you"). Say what happens next and when.
3. Never tell the customer to move money to another account.
4. Cite the help-centre article the answer is based on.
5. Keep replies under 180 words. No internal team names, ticket priorities or risk scores in customer-facing text.
6. Outbound actions that touch the customer or their money (replies, card freezes, refunds) need human approval.
